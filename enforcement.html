Traffic Enforcement Agencies & Legal Framework

Enforcement of Motor Vehicle laws in Jharkhand is carried out under the provisions of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988 and Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019. Two primary enforcement bodies operate in the state:

  • Enforcement Wing of Transport Department: Headed by Motor Vehicle Inspectors (MVIs) and District Transport Officers (DTOs).
  • Traffic Police Department: Managed by District Traffic Police Officers and Superintendents.
