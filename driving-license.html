Driving License Fee Schedule & Criteria

Service Fee (INR) Eligibility Validity
Learner License (LL) Rs. 200 (Rs. 150 LL + Rs. 50 Test Fee) Min Age: 16 (Gearless 50cc), 18 (Non-Transport/Car), 20 (Transport) 6 Months across India
Permanent Driving License (DL) Rs. 700 (Rs. 200 Issue + Rs. 300 Test + Rs. 200 Smart Card) Holder of valid LL for at least 30 days 20 Years or up to age of 40
DL Renewal Rs. 400 (Rs. 200 Renewal + Rs. 200 Smart Card) Expired DL holder within grace period 10 Years / 5 Years (Transport)
Duplicate Driving License Rs. 400 (Rs. 200 Duplicate + Rs. 200 Smart Card) Lost, damaged, or torn smart card DL Co-terminus with original DL
