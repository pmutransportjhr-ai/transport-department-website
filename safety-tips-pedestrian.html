Safety Guidelines for Vulnerable Road Users & School Buses

1. Mandatory School Bus Safety Norms

  • School buses must be painted Golden Yellow with "SCHOOL BUS" written prominently on front and back.
  • Fitted with Speed Governor restricted to maximum 40 km/h.
  • Must have emergency exit doors, fire extinguishers, and first-aid kits.
  • Qualified female attendant mandatory on buses carrying female school students.

2. Pedestrian & Cycling Safety Rules

  • Always use designated footpaths, subways, or zebra crossings to cross roads.
  • Walk facing oncoming traffic if there is no footpath.
  • Cyclists must fit red reflectors on the back of bicycles and keep to the extreme left lane.
