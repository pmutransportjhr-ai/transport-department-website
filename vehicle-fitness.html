Automated Testing Station (ATS) & Fitness Renewal

Under Section 56 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988, a transport vehicle shall not be deemed to be validly registered unless it carries a valid Certificate of Fitness issued by an authorized Automated Testing Station (ATS) or Motor Vehicle Inspector (MVI).

Vehicle Category Initial Fitness Validity Subsequent Renewal Validity
New Commercial Transport Vehicles 2 Years from Date of Registration 1 Year per renewal up to 8 years
Commercial Transport Vehicles (Above 8 Years) 1 Year per renewal Mandatory ATS Automated Inspection
Private Non-Transport Vehicles 15 Years (Included in Initial Registration) 5 Years renewal (FC Extension)
