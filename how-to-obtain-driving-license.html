Procedure for Obtaining Driving License

A Driving License is the most essential and mandatory legal document required to drive any motor vehicle in a public place. Driving Licenses in Jharkhand are processed digitally via the SARATHI 4.0 Portal under the norms set by the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH), Govt. of India.

Apply Online on Sarathi 4.0 Portal

1. Eligibility Criteria for Driving License

  • For Vehicles without Gear (Motorcycle up to 50cc): Applicant must be at least 16 years old (with parental consent).
  • For Light Motor Vehicles (Car, Motorcycle with Gear): Applicant must be at least 18 years old.
  • For Transport / Commercial Vehicles: Applicant must be at least 20 years old and must possess an LMV license for at least 1 year.

2. Step 1: Obtaining a Learner's License (LL)

  1. Visit the online SARATHI portal: parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice.
  2. Select state as Jharkhand and click Apply for Learner License.
  3. Fill in personal details, upload valid Age Proof & Address Proof (Aadhaar, Ration Card, Birth Certificate).
  4. Pay the online LL fee and book an appointment slot for the computer-based LL test at your nearest DTO office.
  5. Appear for the online objective test covering traffic signals, road rules, and signs. Upon passing, the Learner's License is issued on the same day.

3. Step 2: Permanent Driving License (DL)

  1. After 30 days of LL issuance (and within 180 days), apply online for a Permanent Driving License.
  2. Book a practical driving test slot on the Sarathi portal.
  3. Present yourself with your vehicle and original documents at the designated DTO automated driving testing track.
  4. Upon passing the driving test, the smart card DL is printed and dispatched via Speed Post to your registered address within 3 days.
