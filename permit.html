Permit Categories in Jharkhand

Permit Type Applicability Issuing Authority Validity
Stage Carriage Permit Passenger Buses running on designated state/inter-state routes STA / RTA (Ranchi, Dumka, Hazaribagh, Jamshedpur) 5 Years
Contract Carriage Permit Tourist buses, cabs, auto-rickshaws hired under single contract DTO / RTA 5 Years
Goods Carriage Permit Trucks, tippers, and commercial haulers operating within Jharkhand DTO / STA 5 Years
National Permit (Goods) Commercial vehicles carrying goods across all Indian states STA Jharkhand / Parivahan N-Permit Portal 5 Years (Authorization renewal yearly)
All India Tourist Permit (AITP) Luxury passenger vehicles operating nationwide for tourism STA Jharkhand 5 Years
