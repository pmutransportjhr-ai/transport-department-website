Citizen Online Services Guide

Comprehensive instructions for applying online for Driving License, Vehicle Registration (VAHAN), Permits, Road Tax, and Vehicle Certificates in Jharkhand.

How to apply for a Learner License (LL) Online?

  1. Visit the official SARATHI Parivahan Portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in).
  2. Select state as Jharkhand.
  3. Click on Apply for Learner Licence.
  4. Fill in applicant details, upload proof of age & address (Aadhaar/Voter ID), and upload passport-size photo.
  5. Pay the prescribed LL fee online (Rs. 150 LL fee + Rs. 50 test fee).
  6. Book LL computer test slot at your designated District Transport Office (DTO).
  7. Attend test on booked date; upon passing, download instant e-Learner License.

How to apply for Permanent Driving License (DL)?

  1. Permanent DL can be applied after 30 days of LL issuance (within 180 days).
  2. Open SARATHI Portal and click Apply for Driving Licence.
  3. Enter your LL number and date of birth.
  4. Select your vehicle class and book a practical driving track test slot at DTO.
  5. Pay DL fee online (Rs. 200 DL fee + Rs. 300 test fee + Smart card fee).
  6. Present vehicle at DTO track on scheduled date for MVI (Motor Vehicle Inspector) practical test.
  7. Smart-card DL will be dispatched via India Post speed post to your registered address.

How to register a New Motor Vehicle (VAHAN 4.0)?

  1. New vehicle registration is dealer-point automated in Jharkhand.
  2. Authorized motor dealer enters invoice details and uploads Form 20, Form 21, and Form 22 on VAHAN 4.0 portal.
  3. Pay road tax and registration fee online.
  4. High Security Registration Plate (HSRP) is fitted at dealer location.
  5. Digital RC is generated instantly and physical smart card RC is mailed via post.

How to apply for Commercial Stage & Contract Permits online?

Permit applications (Stage Carriage, Goods Carriage, Contract Bus, All India Tourist Permit) are processed through the official State Permit Application Gateway (spermit).
