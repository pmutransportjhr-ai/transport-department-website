Types of Registration Services

Temporary Registration

Issued by authorized dealers for newly purchased vehicles valid for a maximum period of 1 month to enable transit to the destination District Transport Office (DTO).

Requisite Form: Form 20 (Application for Registration)
Permanent Registration

Permanent 15-year registration for non-transport personal vehicles (Cars/Two-wheelers) and valid fitness duration for commercial transport vehicles.

Requisite Forms: Form 20, Form 21 (Sales Certificate), Form 22 (Roadworthiness)
Transfer of Ownership

Required upon sale, inheritance, or auction of motor vehicle. Must be submitted within 30 days of sale.

Requisite Forms: Form 29 & Form 30 (Notice & Application for Transfer)
No Objection Certificate (NOC)

Issued for re-registration or transfer of motor vehicle from Jharkhand to another state or across districts.

Requisite Form: Form 28 (Application for Grant of NOC)
