Good Samaritan Law Protection (Jharkhand Gazette)

Under the Jharkhand Good Samaritan Gazette Guidelines, any person who voluntarily rushes a road accident victim to hospital shall NOT be harassed by police, hospital authorities, or forced to reveal identity. Download: Good Samaritan Gazette Notification PDF.

Primary Causes of Road Crashes & Prevention Strategies

  • Over-speeding: Accounts for 65% of fatal highway crashes. Speed governors are mandatory on commercial trucks and buses.
  • Drunk Driving: Impairs reaction time and judgment. Strict breathalyzer checks are conducted across Jharkhand.
  • Distracted Driving: Using mobile devices or texting while steering.
  • Non-use of Helmets/Seatbelts: Helmets reduce fatal head injury risk by 40%.
