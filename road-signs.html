Standard Indian Road Signs & Traffic Symbols Manual

Road signs are classified into three main statutory categories under the Motor Vehicles Act: Mandatory Signs, Cautionary Warning Signs, and Informatory Signs. Click the tabs below to switch categories.

Mandatory / Regulatory Road Signs

Violation of mandatory road signs is a punishable offense under Section 177 & Section 179 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988.

STOP
Octagonal Red Sign - Full Stop Obligatory Before Proceeding
GIVE WAY
Inverted Triangle - Yield Right of Way to Oncoming Traffic
NO ENTRY
Circular Red Ring - Entry Strictly Prohibited for All Vehicles
ONE WAY TRAFFIC
One Direction Arrow Crossed - Traffic Permitted in One Direction
RIGHT TURN PROHIBITED
Red Bar Across Right Arrow - No Right Turn Allowed
U-TURN PROHIBITED
Curved U-Arrow Crossed Out - U-Turns Strictly Banned
NO HONKING
Horn Prohibited Zone (Quiet Zone near Hospitals & Schools)
SPEED LIMIT (50 km/h)
Red Bordered Number - Maximum Permissible Speed
NO OVERTAKING
Two Overtaking Cars Crossed - Overtaking Strictly Prohibited
LOAD LIMIT (5 TONNES)
Circle with Weight Limit - Maximum Gross Vehicle Weight
HEIGHT LIMIT (3.5 METERS)
Vertical Arrows - Maximum Vehicle Clearance Height
NO PARKING
Blue Circle with Diagonal Red Slash - No Vehicle Parking

Cautionary / Warning Road Signs

Cautionary signs warn drivers of potential hazards ahead so speed can be reduced in advance.

SHARP LEFT TURN
Triangular Warning - Steep Curve Ahead to Left
SHARP RIGHT TURN
Triangular Warning - Steep Curve Ahead to Right
HAIR PIN BEND
U-Turn Bend - Sharp Hairpin Bend Ahead in Mountain/Ghat Roads
PEDESTRIAN CROSSING
Zebra Crossing or School Zone Ahead
SCHOOL AHEAD
School Children Crossing Zone - Reduce Speed to 20 km/h
NARROW BRIDGE
Carriageway Narrows at Upcoming Bridge Passage
SLIPPERY ROAD
Wet, Oily, or Loose Gravel Surface - Drive Cautiously
ROUNDABOUT AHEAD
Circular Junction Ahead - Give Way to Right Traffic
STEEP ASCENT
Steep Uphill Grade - Engage Low Gear
MEN AT WORK
Road Repair / Maintenance Activity - Reduce Speed
UNGUARDED RAILWAY CROSSING
Level Crossing Without Gates 200m Ahead - Stop & Look
SPEED BREAKER / BUMP
Raised Speed Hump Ahead - Slow Down Vehicle

Informatory Road Signs

Informatory signs guide drivers to medical facilities, petrol pumps, parking lots, and destination distances.

HOSPITAL AHEAD
Medical Emergency Center & Trauma Care Nearby
FIRST AID POST
Medical First Aid & Emergency Dispensary
EATING PLACE / RESTAURANT
Highway Food, Meals & Refreshments Service
PARKING FACILITY (P)
Designated Public Vehicle Parking Area
PETROL PUMP / FUEL STATION
24/7 Motor Fuel & EV Charging Station
PUBLIC TELEPHONE
Emergency Highway Phone Booth
RESTING PLACE / HOTEL
Overnight Traveler Lodging & Rest Area
BUS STOP
Designated Passenger Bus Passenger Stop
TOLL PLAZA AHEAD
FASTag Automated Electronic Toll Gate
DESTINATION & DISTANCE
Green Overhead Direction & Kilometer Board
WAY SIDE AMENITIES
Highway Food Court, Restroom & Washroom Plaza
RAILWAY STATION
Connecting Railway Station & Junction
