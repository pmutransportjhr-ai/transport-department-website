Prescribed Statutory Fees under CMV Rules 1989 & Jharkhand MV Rules

Below is the official schedule of fees applicable for various motor vehicle and licensing transactions in District Transport Offices (DTOs) across Jharkhand.

1. Driving License & Learner License Fees

# Purpose Fee (INR) Rule Reference
1 Issue of Learner Licence (LL) for each class of vehicle Rs. 150 Rule 32
2 Learner Licence Driving Test Fee Rs. 50 Rule 32
3 Issue of Driving Licence (DL) Rs. 200 Rule 32
4 Driving Licence Practical Test Fee Rs. 300 Rule 32
5 Smart Card DL Card Fee Rs. 200 State Amendment
