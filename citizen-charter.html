Citizen Charter & Service Delivery Timelines

Mandated turnaround times and prescribed documents for licensing services.

Sr. No Service Form Prescribed Documents Targeted Response Time
1 Learner's Licence Form 2
  1. Valid Age Proof (Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate etc.)
  2. Valid Address Proof (Ration Card, Electricity / Telephone Bill etc.)
  3. Form 1 (Self declaration as to physical fitness)
  4. Form 1A in case of Transport Vehicle Licence
  5. Two Recent Passport size Photographs
 Same Day
2 Driving Licence Form 4
  1. Valid Learner's Licence for the relevant class (> 30 days old)
  2. Three Recent Passport size Photographs
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
3 Renewal of Driving Licence Form 9
  1. Original Driving Licence
  2. Form 1 (Self declaration as to physical fitness)
  3. Form 1A in case of Transport Vehicle Licence
  4. Two Recent Passport size Photographs
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
4 Duplicate Copy of Licence Form L.L.D
  1. Licence Xerox Copy or RTO verified Licence Information
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
5 Change of Address in DL Plain Paper
  1. Original Driving License
  2. Valid Address Proof
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
6 Endorsement in DL (Class Addition) Form 8
  1. Original Driving License
  2. Learner's License of class to be endorsed
  3. Form 1 & Form 1A (for Transport Vehicles)
  4. Training Certificate in Form 5 issued by recognized school
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
7 PSV Badge Authorization Form 8
  1. Original Driving License
  2. Learner's License of relevant Badge
  3. Form 1 & Form 1A
  4. Training Certificate Form 5
  5. Police Station Character/Bonafide Certificate
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
8 International Driving Permit Plain Paper
  1. Copy of Valid Driving Licence
  2. Copy of Valid Air Ticket & Passport
  3. Copy of Valid Visa
  4. Five passport size Photographs
 Same Day
9 Conductor Licence Standard
  1. Mark sheet of 10th Pass or relevant Exam
  2. Character Certificate from Police Station
  3. First Aid Certificate
  4. Two Recent Passport size Photographs
  5. Minimum Age Required: 18 Years
 Sent by Speed Post within 3 Days
